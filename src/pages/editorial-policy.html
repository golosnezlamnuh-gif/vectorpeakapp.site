Editorial policy

{{siteName}} exists to answer one question quickly: what is new on TV and streaming today, and is any of it worth my evening? This page explains how we work, so you can judge how far to trust us.

What we publish

  • Daily listings. The schedule pages are generated from the TVmaze database. We do not type listings by hand and we do not guess times.
  • Editor's notes. A short note for each day with the editor's view of what to watch, what is unusual in the schedule and what can wait.
  • Guides. Longer explainers about how TV schedules, premieres and streaming releases work. They are written to stay useful for months, not for a single day.

We do not write episode reviews or ratings of our own. Where a number such as a rating appears, it comes from TVmaze and is labeled as such.

Where facts come from

Air dates, times, networks, episode numbers, runtimes and descriptions come from TVmaze. Anything else that we state as a fact, for example how a rating system works or when a service usually publishes new episodes, is checked against the organization that owns the fact, such as the network, the streaming service or the rating body. Facts that change often are described as "usually" or "typically", and we point you to the official source for the final answer.

How a piece is made

  1. The editor picks a topic that answers a real question a viewer has.
  2. The facts are collected first, from the sources above.
  3. The text is drafted with the help of AI writing tools.
  4. The editor reads the whole draft, checks every claim that could be wrong, removes what cannot be checked and rewrites anything that does not sound like a person talking to a friend.
  5. The piece is published with its date. If we change it later in a meaningful way, it shows an "Updated" date.

How we use AI

We use AI assistants to help with drafting and editing. We say so because readers have a right to know. Three rules apply to every piece:

  • A person reads, fact-checks and edits the text before it goes live. Nothing is published straight from an AI tool.
  • AI does not supply listings. Times, dates and episode details come from TVmaze, not from a chatbot.
  • We do not use AI to invent quotes, reviews, ratings or personal experiences, and we never claim that someone watched an episode when they did not.

Who writes it

Guides and notes are written and edited by a person. Listings are generated from TVmaze data.

Independence and advertising

Nobody pays to be featured. Which episodes get a brief is decided by a published formula (see How it works), not by networks, studios or advertisers. The site shows ads from Google AdSense. Ads are labeled "Advertisement", kept apart from the text and never decide what we write.The site is free to read and may show advertising in the future. Any ad will be labeled "Advertisement", kept apart from the text, and will never decide what we write. If we ever add affiliate links or sponsored content, they will be marked clearly where they appear.

Spoilers

Episode descriptions are the short descriptions published by the broadcaster or TVmaze. Our own writing avoids spoilers for episodes that have not yet aired in the United States. When a guide has to discuss plot, it says so before it starts.

Corrections

We would rather be told about a mistake than leave it in. If you spot one, write to us on the contact page. We fix errors in our own text as soon as we have checked them, and we add an "Updated" date to the piece. If the mistake is in the underlying listing, we also suggest the fix to TVmaze so that it is corrected for everyone.
