About {{siteName}}

{{siteName}} is an independent daily guide to new TV episodes in English. It shows what airs today, when it airs in your own time zone, and which episodes are worth your evening.

Why it exists

Finding out what is on tonight usually means opening three apps, scrolling past reruns and doing time-zone sums in your head. {{siteName}} puts the new episodes of the day in one timetable, converts the times for you and adds a few lines of context to the ones that matter most, so the decision takes a minute instead of twenty.

What you will find here

  • Today: every new episode of the day on US, UK, Canadian and Australian TV and on streaming services, in one timetable with a live "now" line. The most notable episodes get a short brief.
  • Premieres: new series and returning seasons over the next two weeks.
  • Guides: plain-English explainers on how schedules, premieres, ratings and streaming releases work.
  • Editor's notes: a short note for each day with what to watch tonight and what can wait.
  • My shows: follow shows and see when each one airs next. You can also add episodes to your calendar.
  • Daily archive: a saved copy of each day's listings.

How we work

Listings come from the TVmaze database, not from a chatbot or from guesswork. Our own writing is checked by a person before it is published, and when AI tools help with a draft we say so. The full rules, including how we handle corrections, are in the editorial policy.

How we choose what to feature

Briefs are not paid placements and they are not opinions. They follow a published formula based on popularity, premiere status and ratings. The full method is on the How it works page.
