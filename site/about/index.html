About Cold Open

Cold Open is an independent daily guide to new TV episodes in English. It shows what airs today, when it airs in your own time zone, and which episodes are worth your evening.

What you will find here

  • Today: every new episode of the day on US, UK, Canadian and Australian TV and on streaming services, in one timetable with a live "now" line. The most notable episodes get a short brief.
  • Premieres: new series and returning seasons over the next two weeks.
  • My shows: follow shows and see when each one airs next. You can also add episodes to your calendar.
  • Daily archive: a saved copy of each day's listings.

Who runs it

Cold Open is edited by the Cold Open editor. Questions, corrections and suggestions are welcome on the contact page.

How we choose what to feature

Briefs are not paid placements and they are not opinions. They follow a published formula based on popularity, premiere status and ratings. The full method is on the How it works page.

Independence

Cold Open is not affiliated with any TV network or streaming service. Network, show and service names belong to their owners and are used only to identify programmes. The site is free to read and may show advertising, which never affects which episodes are featured.
