Editor's note

Hi everyone! Streaming leads today, and the best-rated show on the page is Dexter: Resurrection (9.7), episode 5 on Paramount+. Michael C. Hall returns as Dexter while the hunt for the Five Borough Killer (Dan Stevens) intensifies, and Brian Cox plays the New York Ripper. Uma Thurman returns as Charley. It needs the Premium plan, and new episodes land on Fridays. Tulsa King is on the same service: Stallone's season 4 has ten episodes and ends December 18, so after tonight's seventh only three remain.

Football fans get a curveball. The Team: Michigan Football premieres on FOX at 7 PM ET, the night before Michigan plays Ohio State. It's a two-parter from Tom Brady and Jim Harbaugh, who both executive produce. Part one covers Harbaugh through the 2023 title, with Charles Woodson, J.J. McCarthy and Blake Corum. Part two is December 12.

For families, SpongeBob's season 18 opens with an hour-long Christmas special at 5 PM ET: Plankton takes over the North Pole, and Lewis Black voices Santa. It also streams on Paramount+. Grown-ups can try Alan Carr's Great Big Bucket List on Prime Video, four episodes with Jack Dee, Tom Allen and Joanne McNally, reportedly shot in Iceland and the Canary Islands.

Later, Gold Rush is on Discovery at 8 PM ET, and The Rainmaker follows on USA at 10 PM ET, a John Grisham adaptation with Milo Callaghan and John Slattery. My pick: Dexter. Short on time? The Michigan premiere. Enjoy the weekend!
