Editor's note

Hi everyone! The big night is the Dancing with the Stars finale on ABC at 8 PM ET. Five couples, three dances each, one Mirrorball Trophy, and it runs a full 180 minutes, so set the DVR if you can't stay up.

Moonshiners on Discovery (8 PM ET) is the fun one: Tim and Tickle add molasses to their 18th-century mash bill while Jerry opens a new front in his war with Mike. Then at 9 PM ET you've got Road Wars on A&E, The Curse of Oak Island on History and American Masters on PBS, so there's something for every kind of viewer.

The surprise is on streaming. Prime Video lists two Rings of Power episodes today, S03E07 (73 min) and S03E08 (67 min), both marked TBA, so check the app before you plan a binge. Hulu has The Circle S08E04 (rated 7.1 on this page) plus Phony and Reasonable Doubt S04E10.

Late shift: Investigation Discovery debuts I Want to Kill My Boss at 10 PM ET, about an aviation boss at an Arizona aerospace company who gets gunned down. I haven't seen it yet, so no verdict, but true crime fans should give episode one a try. Short on time? Pick the finale for a live winner, or Rings of Power for a binge. Enjoy the night and happy Thanksgiving week!
