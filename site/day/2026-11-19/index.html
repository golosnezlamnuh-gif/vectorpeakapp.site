Editor's note

Hi everyone! Thursday has 44 new episodes, 23 of them on streaming, and four premieres. The big streaming story is The Altruists on Netflix, described as a toxic love story inspired by a real fraud, with all eight episodes listed today. Nelly & Ashanti: We Belong Together is also back on Peacock for season 2, eight half-hour episodes.

On regular TV, my pick is Elsbeth at 9:01 PM ET on CBS. In "Lean Girls," Carrie Preston's Elsbeth goes up against a celebrity fitness guru after a Pilates student is murdered. It is a murder mystery with some humor, so it works even if you missed earlier episodes.

The surprise is ABC running two first-responder shows back to back: 9-1-1 at 8 PM ET, then 9-1-1: Nashville at 9. Also at 9, Next Level Baker returns for season 2 with a holiday-themed episode where Pentatonix performs. Six new episodes start at 9 PM ET, so plan to record one.

I would not marathon The Altruists tonight. Watch episode 1, then decide. At 10 PM ET, Grey's Anatomy (a hospital overrun with patients) and Cupertino (a journalist framed for murder) are on at the same time, so pick one. Enjoy, and see you tomorrow!
