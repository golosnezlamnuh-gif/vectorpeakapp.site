Sunday has 29 new episodes from 28 shows, 4 of them on streaming services. New series: Oobah: Scams for a Better Tomorrow (HBO). Returning: 30 for 30 (ESPN). The busiest slot is 8:00 PM EST, with 6 episodes starting at once. Most popular show on the list: The Simpsons on FOX.

Editor's note

Hi everyone! Sunday is a network night: 29 new episodes, and only 4 on streaming. ESPN opens season 5 of 30 for 30 at 8:30 PM ET, and HBO closes the night at 11 PM ET with the new series Oobah: Scams for a Better Tomorrow. Oobah Butler is known for creating London's #1-rated fake restaurant, and now he goes after the world's biggest industries.

My pick is The Gilded Age at 9 PM ET on HBO. In "Good Taste," Agnes (Christine Baranski) tries to regain control of the dinner, and Peggy's career causes a conflict with William. If you are behind on the season, catch up first.

The surprise is 8 PM ET, when six new episodes start at once. The Simpsons is one of them, with Krusty's latest marriage ending in spectacular fashion, so set the DVR. At 9:30 PM ET, CBS has Tracker, where Justin Hartley's Colter Shaw hunts for a missing inn owner and ends up against a rival tracker.

I would skip the two-hour blocks at 8 PM ET (90 Day Fiance: The Other Way and Tournament of Champions: All-Star Christmas) unless you follow them, because they eat the best part of the evening. Enjoy, and see you tomorrow!
