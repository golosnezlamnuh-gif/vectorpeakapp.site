Privacy policy

Last updated: Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

Cold Open has no accounts and does not ask you for personal information. This page explains what is stored on your device, who else receives data when you use the site, and your choices.

What we store on your device

Your browser keeps a small amount of data on your device so the site can remember you. It is never sent to us.

  • Local storage: the shows you follow and your chosen region. Clear your browser's site data to remove them.
  • Session storage: a short-lived copy of recent listings so pages load faster. It disappears when you close the tab.

Hosting and server logs

The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, for delivery and security. See Cloudflare's own privacy policy for details.

TVmaze

Listings, show details and most posters are loaded directly from TVmaze by your browser. TVmaze therefore receives your IP address and browser details, as any website would when you visit it. See TVmaze's own policy on tvmaze.com.

Advertising

This site may show advertising in the future. If it does, this page will explain which ad providers are used and how to opt out of personalised ads.

Children

Cold Open is a general audience site about television. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no accounts and no personal profiles, there is usually nothing for us to export or delete. You can still ask us a question or make a request about your data at any time through the contact page. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held by the services named above, which you can exercise with them directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
