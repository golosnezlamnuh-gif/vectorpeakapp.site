Saturday, November 28, 2026

Hi everyone! Saturday belongs to broadcast TV: only 3 of the 25 new episodes are on streaming. Early risers get Teen Titans Go! on Cartoon Network at 9 AM ET, with "The Night Begins To Shine - Vinyl City Part 1". Fun fact: that song began in 2005 as a generic '80s-style track for a music library, became a joke on the show, then led to a 2017 four-part special and a 2020 five-part sequel.

For the kitchen, PBS runs America's Test Kitchen at 2:30 PM ET, then Cook's Country at 3:30 PM ET with Glazed Ham and Gratin for a Crowd. The only streaming premiere is Salish's 12 Games of Christmas on Netflix. Salish Matter invites seven friends to a winter cabin, and her dad Jordan Matter hosts twelve challenges while an impostor sabotages the games.

Evening is Hallmark: The Most Wonderful Secret at 6 PM ET with Tammin Sursok and Peter Porte, then Holiday Ever After: A Disney World Wish Come True at 8 PM ET, starring Lacey Chabert and Richard Kind.

For a switch, Svengoolie on Me-TV shows Willard at 8 PM ET, a 1971 cult classic with Bruce Davison as a lonely young man who uses rats for revenge. A&E starts Storage Wars season 19 at 9 PM ET, rated 5.9 on this page. My pick: Hallmark, then Svengoolie. Enjoy the weekend!

Friday, November 27, 2026

Hi everyone! Streaming leads today, and the best-rated show on the page is Dexter: Resurrection (9.7), episode 5 on Paramount+. Michael C. Hall returns as Dexter while the hunt for the Five Borough Killer (Dan Stevens) intensifies, and Brian Cox plays the New York Ripper. Uma Thurman returns as Charley. It needs the Premium plan, and new episodes land on Fridays. Tulsa King is on the same service: Stallone's season 4 has ten episodes and ends December 18, so after tonight's seventh only three remain.

Football fans get a curveball. The Team: Michigan Football premieres on FOX at 7 PM ET, the night before Michigan plays Ohio State. It's a two-parter from Tom Brady and Jim Harbaugh, who both executive produce. Part one covers Harbaugh through the 2023 title, with Charles Woodson, J.J. McCarthy and Blake Corum. Part two is December 12.

For families, SpongeBob's season 18 opens with an hour-long Christmas special at 5 PM ET: Plankton takes over the North Pole, and Lewis Black voices Santa. It also streams on Paramount+. Grown-ups can try Alan Carr's Great Big Bucket List on Prime Video, four episodes with Jack Dee, Tom Allen and Joanne McNally, reportedly shot in Iceland and the Canary Islands.

Later, Gold Rush is on Discovery at 8 PM ET, and The Rainmaker follows on USA at 10 PM ET, a John Grisham adaptation with Milo Callaghan and John Slattery. My pick: Dexter. Short on time? The Michigan premiere. Enjoy the weekend!

Sunday, November 15, 2026

# Sunday TV Picks: November 15 — Three Episodes, One Big Night

The biggest highlight today is **The Chosen** on Prime Video. Season 6 opens with three episodes, giving viewers a chance to experience a major turning point in the story without waiting a week between chapters. Jonathan Roumie returns as Jesus, bringing emotional depth to a series built around faith, difficult choices and human relationships. This is the day's standout streaming event.

For fans of modern Western crime drama, **Marshals** is worth watching. Luke Grimes returns as Kayce Dutton in the Yellowstone universe, while this episode gives Andrea a more prominent role as the team faces a search for a missing nephew. Later, **The Gilded Age** offers a completely different kind of drama: Carrie Coon and Morgan Spector lead a story of ambition, wealth and family tensions. It's the strongest choice for viewers who enjoy sophisticated period dramas.

The most interesting part of the evening is the crowded lineup of **The Gilded Age, The Institute, Tracker and Maigret**. Each offers a different experience: social intrigue, psychological suspense, a dangerous investigation and classic detective storytelling. Benjamin Wainwright's Maigret is especially worth considering for mystery fans, although its story continues in Part Two. Meanwhile, Justin Hartley and Fiona Rene give Tracker an appealing character-driven angle.
