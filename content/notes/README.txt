Editor's notes (optional, but the best thing you can add for readers and for Google)

Create a plain-text file named after the day, in US Eastern date form:  2026-11-10.txt
Write 2 to 4 short paragraphs in English: what you would watch tonight and why, what surprised you
in the schedule, what to skip. A blank line starts a new paragraph. Plain text only: no HTML.

The next daily build shows the note under the summary at the top of that day's front page,
and keeps it on the permanent page /day/2026-11-10/.   Maximum: 4 paragraphs, 1,500 characters.
Notes are text you write yourself, so they are the one part of the site that is truly your own content.
