Hi everyone! Saturday belongs to broadcast TV: only 3 of the 25 new episodes are on streaming. Early risers get Teen Titans Go! on Cartoon Network at 9 AM ET, with "The Night Begins To Shine - Vinyl City Part 1". Fun fact: that song began in 2005 as a generic '80s-style track for a music library, became a joke on the show, then led to a 2017 four-part special and a 2020 five-part sequel.

For the kitchen, PBS runs America's Test Kitchen at 2:30 PM ET, then Cook's Country at 3:30 PM ET with Glazed Ham and Gratin for a Crowd. The only streaming premiere is Salish's 12 Games of Christmas on Netflix. Salish Matter invites seven friends to a winter cabin, and her dad Jordan Matter hosts twelve challenges while an impostor sabotages the games.

Evening is Hallmark: The Most Wonderful Secret at 6 PM ET with Tammin Sursok and Peter Porte, then Holiday Ever After: A Disney World Wish Come True at 8 PM ET, starring Lacey Chabert and Richard Kind.
