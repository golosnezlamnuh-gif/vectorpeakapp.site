Hey everyone! If your looking for something good to watch this Tuesday, CBS is basically owning the night with a massive *NCIS* franchise triple-header. While the original *NCIS* (Season 24) puts Nick Torres—played by *That '70s Show* alum Wilmer Valderrama—in a high-stakes undercover getaway car situation, the real must-watch is *NCIS: Origins* (Season 3). Austin Stowell continues to nail the younger, grittier Leroy Jethro Gibbs—a role Mark Harmon made iconic—and tonight's 1993 flashback dives straight into the mystery of what happened to Lala.

If procedurals aren't your vibe tonight, *Dancing with the Stars* Season 35 is hitting its high-pressure Semi-Finals on ABC, where couples have to pull off both ballroom and Latin routines to survive. Over on Hulu, *Reasonable Doubt* (Season 4) is dropping a new episode, and Emayatzy Corinealdi remains one of the most underrated leads on TV right now. On the flip side, you can probably skip *7 Little Johnstons* Season 18 premiere on TLC unless your deeply invested in the family's long-running reality drama, as it feels a bit repetitive at this point.

The biggest surprise on today's schedule has to be *The Circle* returning for Season 8 on Hulu instead of its usual Netflix home, which is a pretty wild platform shift for the social media catfish competition. Also, Bobby Flay is kicking off Season 5 of *Beat Bobby Flay: Holiday Throwdown* with Brooke Williamson and Damaris Phillips, giving us an early dose of festive food chaos.
