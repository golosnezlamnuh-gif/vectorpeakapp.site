# Sunday TV Picks: November 15 — Three Episodes, One Big Night

The biggest highlight today is **The Chosen** on Prime Video. Season 6 opens with three episodes, giving viewers a chance to experience a major turning point in the story without waiting a week between chapters. Jonathan Roumie returns as Jesus, bringing emotional depth to a series built around faith, difficult choices and human relationships. This is the day's standout streaming event.

For fans of modern Western crime drama, **Marshals** is worth watching. Luke Grimes returns as Kayce Dutton in the Yellowstone universe, while this episode gives Andrea a more prominent role as the team faces a search for a missing nephew. Later, **The Gilded Age** offers a completely different kind of drama: Carrie Coon and Morgan Spector lead a story of ambition, wealth and family tensions. It's the strongest choice for viewers who enjoy sophisticated period dramas.

The most interesting part of the evening is the crowded lineup of **The Gilded Age, The Institute, Tracker and Maigret**. Each offers a different experience: social intrigue, psychological suspense, a dangerous investigation and classic detective storytelling. Benjamin Wainwright's Maigret is especially worth considering for mystery fans, although its story continues in Part Two. Meanwhile, Justin Hartley and Fiona Rene give Tracker an appealing character-driven angle.

**What can you skip?** Skip the familiar reality and repeat-style options if you're looking for something fresh. Grimsburg and Sister Wives may suit regular fans, but tonight's stronger choices offer more compelling reasons to tune in. One more surprise: the short WNBA Draft listing makes this a good night for basketball fans to keep an eye on the league's next generation.

Have a good Sunday!
