# Sunday TV Guide: November 15, 2026 — The Chosen’s Big Return, a Yellowstone Twist, and a WNBA Surprise

Sunday’s lineup has more going on than a routine night of reruns and weekly episodes. There is a major three-episode streaming launch, a meaningful shift in the *Yellowstone* universe, and a sports event that the schedule actually labels incorrectly. The biggest challenge tonight is not finding something to watch — it is choosing between several strong options airing at the same time.

## The biggest story of the day: *The Chosen* returns with three episodes

**The Chosen — Season 6 premiere (Prime Video)**

The most significant release in the all-day section is undoubtedly *The Chosen*. Season 6 begins a particularly consequential chapter: the story follows Jesus’ final day through the perspectives of those who love him and those who condemn him.

The first episode, “Dawning,” sets a tense tone. The disciples are struggling with fear, confusion and betrayal, while Nicodemus faces a confrontation with the High Priest. Jonathan Roumie remains the central presence, supported by familiar cast members including Shahar Isaac and Elizabeth Tabish.

Here is the detail viewers should not miss: this is not a one-episode premiere. **Prime Video is releasing the first three episodes on November 15**, with the remaining episodes arriving weekly through December 6. That makes today a particularly good opportunity for viewers who prefer to watch a story develop over several episodes rather than wait a week after the opening chapter.

**Verdict:** The strongest streaming event in the schedule, especially for viewers already invested in the characters and the series’ historical and religious drama.

## The best evening choices

### 7:00–8:00 PM ET — A light start, with an unexpected sports event

**America’s Funniest Home Videos (ABC)** is the easy family-friendly choice. Tonight’s episode brings together chaotic cats, sibling showdowns, macho mishaps and mechanic mishaps. Hosted by Alfonso Ribeiro, the show remains a reliable option when the goal is uncomplicated entertainment rather than a complicated new storyline.

But there is a more unusual choice at 7:30 PM: the listing calls it the **WNBA Draft**, although the event is actually the **2027 WNBA Draft Lottery**. That distinction matters. This is not the player draft itself; it is the lottery that helps determine the order of the first picks next spring. Houston, Los Angeles, Portland, Seattle, Toronto and Washington are among the participating teams.

For basketball fans, this short ESPN special may be more consequential than its modest listing suggests.

### 8:00 PM ET — *Marshals*: the Yellowstone connection gets more interesting

**Marshals — “Roughstock” (CBS)**

For fans of *Yellowstone*, this is one of the night's strongest scripted choices. Luke Grimes returns as Kayce Dutton, but the interesting development is that the episode gives Ash Santos’s Andrea a chance to step into a leadership role while Cal is away.

Kayce, meanwhile, goes beyond the Marshals’ jurisdiction to help Mo search for his missing nephew. The episode also develops the relationship between Cal and Belle during their trip to Utah.

That combination makes the hour more than another case-of-the-week story. It tests the team's relationships and puts pressure on characters who do not always get to lead.

**Verdict:** A strong pick for *Yellowstone* viewers and anyone who enjoys character-driven crime drama. Newcomers may find the family and team relationships easier to appreciate with some background on the series.

### 8:30 PM ET — *The Simpsons* turns Moe’s sleep problem into a nightmare

**The Simpsons — “Dream Scenari-Moe” (FOX)**

Moe signs up for a new sleep study and begins experiencing the romantic-comedy dreams he apparently never gets in real life. Naturally, the fantasy comes with a nightmarish price.

This is a fun change of pace after the more serious drama on CBS. The premise also gives the episode a chance to play with the familiar contrast between Moe’s grumpy everyday personality and the romantic world of his dreams.

**Verdict:** The best lighter scripted option of the evening, particularly for longtime fans of the show’s character-driven humor.

## 9:00 PM ET — Four shows compete for your attention

This is the most difficult decision in the entire lineup. *The Gilded Age*, *The Institute*, *Tracker* and *Maigret* all offer very different reasons to stay tuned.

**Best ensemble drama: The Gilded Age — “Ain’t That Good News” (HBO)**

If you prefer ambitious characters, social maneuvering and excellent period production, this is the evening’s strongest choice. Carrie Coon’s Bertha Russell and Morgan Spector’s George remain central to the drama of wealth, influence and the cost of ambition. Christine Baranski and Cynthia Nixon bring a different kind of authority to the series’ established New York society.

Tonight, the Russell family must deal with the consequences of Bertha’s actions, while George faces pressure over the future of his company.

The appeal of Julian Fellowes’s series is not simply its costumes or lavish settings. It is the way personal relationships become battles over status, money and control.

**Verdict:** My top choice for viewers who want a richly acted, carefully constructed drama.

**Best dark thriller: The Institute — “The Sanctuary” (MGM+)**

Ben Barnes and Mary-Louise Parker lead this darker science-fiction and horror series about gifted children caught up in the plans of a mysterious organization.

Tonight, the crew is on the run, the Institute is struggling after a setback, and Luke faces a decision that could cost him everything. The episode sounds especially promising for viewers who enjoy psychological tension and morally complicated characters.

One caution: this is Season 2, Episode 2. It is not the ideal place for a completely new viewer to begin.

**Verdict:** Choose this if you are already following the story and want something unsettling rather than comforting.

**Best crime-series option: Tracker — “Blind Spot” (CBS)**

Justin Hartley’s Colter Shaw is back, but tonight's case gives Reenie, played by Fiona Rene, a more personal connection to the investigation. She asks Colter to help find her mentor’s estranged daughter, and the search develops into a dangerous conspiracy.

That focus on Reenie is the episode’s most interesting angle. It gives the supporting character more emotional weight instead of making the entire hour another solo showcase for Colter.

**Verdict:** The best choice for viewers who want a fast-moving mystery with a familiar procedural structure.

**Best traditional detective mystery: Maigret — “The Yellow Dog, Part One” (PBS)**

Benjamin Wainwright leads this contemporary adaptation of Georges Simenon’s famous detective stories. Tonight, a wealthy businessman is murdered, and Maigret begins to suspect that the key to the case lies in an investigation from 15 years earlier.

The important detail is in the title: this is **Part One**. The story is not designed to deliver its complete resolution in a single evening. If you enjoy patient detective work, clues and a longer investigation, this is a good choice. Viewers who want a complete mystery tonight may prefer *Tracker*.

**Verdict:** A particularly good option for classic crime-fiction fans who do not mind waiting for the second half.

## 9:30–10:00 PM ET — What is worth keeping, and what can wait?

**Grimsburg — “Evidence Locker II” (FOX)**

Jon Hamm’s animated detective comedy gets a reflective episode as the characters revisit their past while going through the evidence locker. It could be entertaining for regular viewers, but the premise is less immediately compelling than tonight’s new dramatic cases.

**Sister Wives (TLC)**

A reasonable late-night choice for viewers who already follow the series. However, tonight’s listing does not reveal a particularly strong standalone hook, so it is not where I would begin if I were choosing only one new show to sample.

## My final recommendations

If you only have time for one programme, choose **The Gilded Age** for its ensemble acting and character drama, or **Marshals** if you prefer a more direct, action-oriented story.

For a streaming event rather than a single television episode, choose **The Chosen** — the three-episode launch makes it the most substantial release of the day.

And if basketball is your interest, do not overlook the WNBA Draft Lottery. The schedule’s label is misleading, but the actual event has real implications for the league’s next season.

**The real lesson from tonight’s lineup:** do not try to watch everything. The 9 PM hour is unusually crowded, so choose the kind of story you want — period drama, suspense, a detective mystery or a contemporary crime series — and make that your main event.
