Hi everyone! Saturday is a quiet one: 18 new episodes, and only one of them on streaming. That one is the series premiere of Editorially Speaking on Tubi, a drama-comedy about journalists at a fashion magazine. Tubi is free with ads, so the first episode costs you nothing.

Daytime belongs to public TV. Five days before Thanksgiving, Cook's Country serves "Cookies for the Holidays" at 3:30 PM ET on PBS, and America's Test Kitchen is on an hour earlier. Woodsmith Shop and Milk Street fill the morning and early afternoon if you have a weekend project.

My evening pick is Svengoolie at 8 PM ET on Me-TV. Tonight's movie is Cujo, the Stephen King story about a rabid Saint Bernard, and the Sven Squad adds movie facts and plenty of goofiness. It runs two hours, so it is a good choice if you like horror but do not want it too serious.

For true crime, Prosecuting Evil with Kelly Siegler is at 9 PM ET, followed by Homicide for the Holidays at 10 on Oxygen. I would skip On Patrol: Live, which takes three hours at 9 PM ET, unless you like police ride-alongs. Enjoy, and see you tomorrow!
